Our story

The short version

Loom was founded on a simple observation: most people are not looking for the cheapest option, they are looking for someone who will do what they said they would.

We have kept the team small and senior so that the person who takes your call is the person who does the work, and we quote fixed fees so you are never watching a meter.

Most of our work now comes from clients we have had for years, which is the only endorsement we really trust.
